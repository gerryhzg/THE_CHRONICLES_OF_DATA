# Chapter One quest flow

Chapter One starts with a practical need: Harvest Village cannot protect its crops by trusting a dry forecast while rain is falling. Each investigation helps someone make a better decision, then provides a reason to visit the next place. **Act I, The Broken Forecast, is playable from its opening through the council and forest departure.** The existing forest investigation remains playable afterward. The full chapter's Archive repair is still planned.

## Playable Act I and forest continuation

The required Act I path is **broken forecast → Lyra → three historical records in any order → Weather Tower comparison → DataDex → all three demonstrations → choose one starter → Kai → village council → northern gate**. The investigation comes before the starter. Basic Observation and Scan are available with the DataDex; advanced Pattern Sight remains a later reward. There are no mandatory calculations or knowledge quizzes.

The forest continuation follows **Inferra's assignment → six habitat/time records → review the finding → report to Lyra**. It remains a separate investigation and a closure for the currently playable journey, rather than the Chapter One ending. Earlier valid saves retain their existing starter, forest access, records, and report progress; migration does not force the new opening onto an established journey.

Optional activities remain available without changing the main ending:

- **Village discoveries:** recover and return the child's Lost Scarf for 15 coins; read the old Observer journal for an Archive Fragment; find the supply chest behind the shop; clear the flooded garden for research points. Rewards occur once. None gates the council or forest road.
- **Voltkit recruitment:** after Kai, visit the village grass or forest Spark Glade, scan, weaken Voltkit, and use a Bond Crystal. Fleeing, defeat, or an unsuccessful bond permits another attempt; sanctuary supplies support recovery. Recruitment is a side adventure, not proof that the investigation is complete.
- **Sampling mastery:** after the finding, help Inferra plan a more representative follow-up expedition. The extra reflection advances DataDex mastery and awards 10 research points once. It does not gate the report or require calculation.
- **Village services:** return home to rest and save, use the sanctuary to care for companions, review the DataDex, and buy supplies from Nell. Healing potions cost 12 coins, Bond Crystals 20, and antidotes 10. Required progression does not depend on purchases.

## Full Chapter One sequence and dependencies

The following reviews all fourteen quests in the original design. **C1-04 through C1-14 are plans, not playable features.** Their completion conditions below make the intended design concrete; they are not claims about current engine support. C1-01 and C1-02 now form the complete Act I; the report to Lyra after C1-03 remains a playable closure rather than a replacement for the full chapter ending.

The proposed main sequence is **C1-01 → C1-02 → C1-03 → C1-04 → C1-05 → C1-06 → C1-07 → C1-08 → C1-09 → C1-10 → C1-11 → C1-12 → C1-13 → C1-14**. Within a region, collection tasks can remain flexible; require earlier results only when the next decision actually needs them.

This plan awards it after the first modeling activity so it can support C1-07; the first seal remains the act-end reward. Distribution Sight similarly belongs at the histogram gate so the next battle can use it.

## Clarifying the Archive seals

The source requires three seals from Merchant City, Probability Woods, and the Mountains, then calls C1-13 **The Six Archive Seals**. The design decision is **three physical access seals plus six components of the Archive review**, rather than six additional collectible items. Preserve C1-13's identity while clarifying its displayed title and objectives. The ending's proposed **six other Archives** is a separate future-world hook, not a Chapter One quest prerequisite.

## Guidance, feedback, and continuity

Objectives should answer three questions: **What do I do? Where do I go? Why does it matter?** A finished collection objective should change to review or reporting, rather than continuing to say “collect evidence.” Completion should name the changed situation, the reward actually earned, and the next destination.

For Nocturnal, hint progression moves from the ranger's missing perspective, to the player's missing habitat/time combinations, to using the campfire and coverage grid. Once all six are recorded, hints switch to comparison. A nocturnal overgeneralization receives daytime evidence; a certainty claim receives the distinction between sightings and complete knowledge. Records remain available after an incorrect finding. Optional mastery gives its own feedback and does not reopen the main quest.

Use the same pattern for future regions: navigation help before conceptual explanation, then evidence-specific guidance. Include recoverable defeat, repeatable observation review, and dialogue appropriate to returning players. Characters keep the speaking styles in [character-voices.md](character-voices.md); disagreement should expose a method's limits without humiliating the person who used it.

The engine owns saved progress, unlocks, and one-time rewards. `dist/quests.js` derives current guidance and main journey steps; dialogue, journal, and markers present the same state. Keep stable IDs and migrate older saves when persistent fields change. Validate premature completion, retries, reward replay, and save/load as well as the successful route. The reusable authoring skill is [data-quest-design](../skills/data-quest-design/SKILL.md).
