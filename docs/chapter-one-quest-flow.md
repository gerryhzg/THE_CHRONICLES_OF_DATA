# Chapter One quest flow

Chapter One starts with a practical need: Harvest Village cannot protect its crops by trusting a dry forecast while rain is falling. Each investigation should help someone make a better decision, then provide a reason to visit the next place. The full chapter ends with repairing the Archive; the current Stage 1 prototype ends much earlier, when the Observer brings the forest finding home to Lyra.

## Playable Stage 1

The required path is **Lyra and one starter → Kai → Inferra's assignment → six habitat/time records → review the finding → report to Lyra**. Voltkit recruitment and the mastery reflection are optional activities.

| Step | Why it matters | What the player does | Completion and next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The broken forecast | Rowan's seedlings and the village's food are at risk. The Oracle predicted 0 mm; the gauge recorded 46 mm. | Meet Lyra at the village laboratory and choose one of three companions. The Weather Tower provides the forecast comparison. | A starter and DataDex begin the journey. Go to Kai in the village square to prepare for fieldwork. |
| The First Bond, C1-02 | A new Observer needs a partner and practice before leaving home. | Win the friendly battle with Kai. Try moves, Guard, and the free scan; the scan is available once per encounter without spending a turn. | Kai's defeat completes First Bond and opens the forest road. A loss allows recovery and another attempt. Take the eastern forest road, then visit Inferra at the field station. |
| The Nocturnal Mystery, C1-03: accept | The village needs trustworthy observations. Inferra wants to test a sweeping claim made from a ranger's night patrol. | Speak with Inferra at the forest field station and accept the assignment. | The investigation becomes active. Visit the meadow, riverbank, and canopy. |
| The Nocturnal Mystery: observe | A claim about a whole forest should not rest on one corner of it at night. | Record all three habitats in daylight and moonlight. Use the campfire to change time. The investigation grid shows missing combinations. | Six unique habitat/time records make the evidence ready for review. These are six observation sessions, not six creatures. Return to the evidence with Inferra. |
| The Nocturnal Mystery: decide | The player can now see what the original records left out. | Compare the ranger's canopy-at-night sightings with the broader records. Submit the finding that the original sample excluded relevant habitats and times. | The investigation completes; the Sampling Compass, 20 research points, and 40 coins are awarded once. Wrong findings retain the records and receive specific feedback. Return to Lyra in Harvest Village. |
| Bring the finding home | Research matters when it changes how people act. Lyra needs the method and its limits, not another confident claim. | Speak with Lyra after the investigation and report the finding. | The separate report completes the Stage 1 main journey. The forest sample does not prove deliberate corruption or repair the Oracle. Further Chapter One regions remain planned. |

The opening adapts **C1-01, The Missing Rainfall Records** into a forecast-and-gauge comparison. Its original three-record village search is not implemented. Do not present it as a completed collection quest or require nonexistent records to leave the village.

Optional activities remain available without changing the main ending:

- **Voltkit recruitment:** after Kai, find the Spark Glade, scan, weaken Voltkit, and use a Bond Crystal. Fleeing, defeat, or an unsuccessful bond permits another attempt; sanctuary supplies support recovery. Recruitment is a side adventure, not proof that the forest investigation is complete.
- **Sampling mastery:** after the finding, help Inferra plan a more representative follow-up expedition. The extra reflection advances DataDex mastery and awards 10 research points once. It does not gate the report or require calculation.
- **Weather Tower, sanctuary, and DataDex:** review the discrepancy, restore the team, replenish supplies, or revisit observations at a useful moment.

## Full Chapter One sequence and dependencies

The following reviews all fourteen quests in the original design. **C1-04 through C1-14 are plans, not playable features.** Their completion conditions below make the intended design concrete; they are not claims about current engine support. C1-01 is only adapted in Stage 1, and the report to Lyra is a prototype closure rather than a replacement for the full chapter ending.

The proposed main sequence is **C1-01 → C1-02 → C1-03 → C1-04 → C1-05 → C1-06 → C1-07 → C1-08 → C1-09 → C1-10 → C1-11 → C1-12 → C1-13 → C1-14**. Within a region, collection tasks can remain flexible; require earlier results only when the next decision actually needs them.

| --- | --- | --- |

Award the DataDex, then visit Lyra's companions. Stage 1 uses the shorter adaptation above. |

Observe Variantor Prime's steady and volatile attack modes and adapt defensive choices. | Resolve the guardian encounter and restore the observatory. Award the third physical seal and the historical record linking discrepancies to altered records. With three seals, proceed to the Grand Archive. Ordinary combat remains viable. |

Define the decision, collect trustworthy records, analyze, model, quantify uncertainty, and summarize. | Restore all six review components and resolve inconsistent records. Apply observation, variation, and conditional evidence across the three battle phases. | Defeat the Warden, restore the deleted records, and show the Oracle recalculating with acknowledged uncertainty. The source places Model Lens at both the merchant act's conclusion and the C1-06 registry reward. This plan awards it after the first modeling activity so it can support C1-07; the first seal remains the act-end reward. Distribution Sight similarly belongs at the histogram gate so the next battle can use it.

## Clarifying the Archive seals

The source requires three seals from Merchant City, Probability Woods, and the Mountains, then calls C1-13 **The Six Archive Seals**. The design decision is **three physical access seals plus six components of the Archive review**, rather than six additional collectible items. Preserve C1-13's identity while clarifying its displayed title and objectives. The ending's proposed **six other Archives** is a separate future-world hook, not a Chapter One quest prerequisite.

## Guidance, feedback, and continuity

Objectives should answer three questions: **What do I do? Where do I go? Why does it matter?** A finished collection objective should change to review or reporting, rather than continuing to say “collect evidence.” Completion should name the changed situation, the reward actually earned, and the next destination.

For Nocturnal, hint progression moves from the ranger's missing perspective, to the player's missing habitat/time combinations, to using the campfire and coverage grid. Once all six are recorded, hints switch to comparison. A nocturnal overgeneralization receives daytime evidence; a certainty claim receives the distinction between sightings and complete knowledge. Records remain available after an incorrect finding. Optional mastery gives its own feedback and does not reopen the main quest.

Use the same pattern for future regions: navigation help before conceptual explanation, then evidence-specific guidance. Include recoverable defeat, repeatable observation review, and dialogue appropriate to returning players. Characters keep the speaking styles in [character-voices.md](character-voices.md); disagreement should expose a method's limits without humiliating the person who used it.

The engine owns saved progress, unlocks, and one-time rewards. `dist/quests.js` derives current guidance and main journey steps; dialogue, journal, and markers present the same state. Keep stable IDs and migrate older saves when persistent fields change. Validate premature completion, retries, reward replay, and save/load as well as the successful route. The reusable authoring skill is [data-quest-design](../skills/data-quest-design/SKILL.md).
